RECOMMENDED DIRECTION

Make it rich.
Ship it light.

Blender for the things you can shoot. Author consistent models and lighting once. Use detailed PBR meshes, real tumbling, shadow maps and impact debris in Ultra. Export small rotation atlases for lower tiers.

Sunburst for the things you fly past. Create detailed source plates, then serve a 960, 1600 or 2560 px background. Keep the play area dark enough to read bullets and targets. This concept uses the available image tool: its source is 1672 × 941, and its underlying model is not exposed.

Spend detail before frame time. Ultra uses live 3D lighting, cast and received shadows, subtle bloom, and pooled collision chips and dust. Balanced and Low use cached 32-frame Blender atlases. These are controlled art and collision fixtures; older phones still need real sustained-play acceptance.
